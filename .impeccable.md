## Design Context

### Users
Facility operators monitor shrimp tanks and water conditions. Account access should be clear and quick on phones and desktop computers.

### Brand Personality
Calm, practical, natural. Preserve the existing EcoPrawn identity shown in the user's reference.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm sand backgrounds, white cards, deep green text, serif display headings, and compact monospace labels. Reuse existing CSS tokens and rounded controls.

### Design Principles
- Keep account forms readable with visible labels and helpful errors.
- Preserve keyboard focus and mobile usability.
- Explain verification steps and resend timing clearly.
- Keep administrative and implementation details out of normal account flows.
